🌱 The Onto-Epistemic Tree of Revelation

A recursive cosmology rooted in the God-trunk

Fusing Bayesian humility, Kabbalistic architecture, and Git metaphysics

🌊 Roots – Chaos / Pre-Signal
The void. The tohu wa-bohu. Not absence, but overpresence—an unfiltered torrent of possible worlds. Here is noise, the precondition for all signal.
"In the beginning, there was no beginning. There was only potential."
❤️ Soil – Love as Signal Attractor
Roots don't grow in air. They bind to something. Love is what grips the real—what chooses this patch of earth.
"Faith is not certainty, it is commitment to cultivation."
🪵 Trunk – The Judaic God
Here, form stabilizes. The Trunk is monotheism as memory—vertical structure that holds together root and branch. God is the constraint that keeps the world coherent, while allowing divergence above.
"I Am Who I Am" is a recursive declaration. Not essence, but becoming."
🌀 Fork – Prophets, Mystics, Rebels
Here begins divergence—not rebellion from God, but differentiation through God. Forks are interpretive paths: Moses, Isaiah, Spinoza, Einstein. Each forks from the Trunk—but contains its sap.
"The fear of the Lord is the beginning of wisdom, but not its end."
🐬 Branch – Law, Culture, Lives
What is lived becomes the branch. Rituals. Stories. Generations. This is where metaphysics touches reality, and also where entropy creeps in. Branches sometimes rot.
"Prune the branch, not the root."
🔁 Recurse – Grace, Teshuvah, Evolution
Some branches die. But recursion means: they don't have to stay dead. God may fell the trunk, but nearby trees—the community—offer graft points. Support. Nutrient flows. Resurrection via communion.
"A bruised reed he will not break."
Bayesian Beauty
Forget Kuhn's revolutions. Science doesn't need rupture—it thrives on recursive priors. Each update isn't a break from the past, it's a deepening spiral.
Kuhn was a dramatist. Bayes is the prophet of continuity.
